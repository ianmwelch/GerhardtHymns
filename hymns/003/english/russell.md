*Translation of stanzas 1–3, and 5.*  
<br>

1. Immanuel, we sing to thee,  
   Pure source of life and love most free:  
   Our light most fair, our morning star,  
   Lord of all lords and lands afar.
1. To thee with angel-hosts we sing,  
   To thee our highest praise we bring:  
   That thou, our long-expected Lord,  
   Dost give to faith its bright reward.  
1. Lo! in the ages far away,  
   The hearts of thine desir'd this day:  
   This hope through ages did inspire  
   Our fathers with divine desire.
1. O that the Lord our bands would break,  
   And bring us aid for Zion's sake;  
   When He to Zion shall descend,  
   Then Israel's joy shall know no end.
