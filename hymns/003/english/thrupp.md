*Translation of stanzas 1–3, 6, and 20.*  
<br>

1. Immanuel, to Thee we sing,  
   Of life and love th' eternal Spring:  
    Thou Light of Heav'n, Thou Star of Morn,  
   Lord of all lords, yet virgin-born.  
1. ﻿﻿To Thee, with angel-hosts; we raise  
   Our noblest strains of grateful praise,  
   That Thou, our long expected Guest,  
   Hast deign'd with us on earth to rest.
1. ﻿﻿Lo, in the ages far away,  
    Full many a heart desir'd this day:  
    For this in hope the Prophets yearn'd,  
   This Patriarchs old in faith discern'd.  
1. ﻿﻿And now, O Saviour, Thou art come,  
   The manger is Thy lowly home:  
   At Bethl'hem's inn a stranger Thou,  
   Though all the heav'ns before Thee bow!  
1. For this will we, to Thee our King,  
   Our joyful hallelujahs sing,  
   With angel-choirs who Thee adore  
   In realms of light for evermore.
